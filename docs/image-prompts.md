## TWO RULES THAT DECIDE EVERYTHING

**1. The subject is the extract, not the plant.**
ARN sells standardised extracts, phytochemicals, oils and oleoresins — the
refined, assayed output. So the hero of every shot is the **finished material**:
fine powder, crystalline isolate, amber oil, viscous resin, in laboratory and
pharmaceutical-grade vessels. A leaf or root may appear only as a small
provenance cue at the edge of frame. Piles of whole spices make ARN look like a
spice trader, which undersells the business.

**2. Premium means light, not dark.**
Bright, clean and luminous — think high-end skincare and luxury pharmacy, not
a moody restaurant. Soft diffused daylight, gentle shadows, airy space. Luxury
comes from beautiful materials, precision and restraint, not from turning the
lights off.

---

## HOW TO USE

1. Paste the **prompt**, then the **STYLE BLOCK** below it. Always both.
2. Ask GPT for the **"Generate at"** size — those are the only sizes it outputs.
3. Upscale 2× and crop to the **"Final file"** size. For banners crop off the
   top and bottom, never the sides.
4. Save to `public/images/` with the filename shown.

## Tips

1. Ask for **3 variations** and pick the one with the **cleanest composition** —
   not the darkest.
2. If a result comes back murky, heavy or moody, add: *"brighter overall
   exposure, soft diffused daylight, light airy background, open gentle
   shadows."*
3. If GPT gives you a pile of whole spices or a bunch of fresh herbs,
   regenerate and add: *"the subject is refined processed extract powder in
   laboratory vessels, not raw plant material."*
4. Keep the **same stone surface** across the five product cards or the grid
   won't read as one set.
5. Never accept text or labels on the vials — regenerate rather than crop.
